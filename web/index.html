requirements:
  - label: No stairs
    importance: INF            # 0..1, or INF = must-have
    flexibility: 0.0           # 0..1 tolerance
    condition: {property: D_STAIRS, op: EQUALS, value: false}
  - label: Away from tram tracks
    importance: 0.7
    flexibility: 0.4
    condition:
      NOT:                     # AND / OR (list), NOT (one)
        property: D_TRAM
        op: EQUALS             # EQUALS | LESSER_THAN | GREATER_THAN
        value: "yes"
        distance: {op: LESSER_THAN, metres: 30}
Importance ≥ 0.9 with flexibility ≤ 0.1 is also treated as must-have. EQUALS on amenities (toilets, bins, benches…) becomes a "go via" stop. See the Properties tab for what has data.
